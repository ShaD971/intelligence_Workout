Score behavior
- Facile starts at 120 points, loses 4 points per row or column move, and receives a moderate 60 point victory bonus.
- Moyen starts at 220 points, loses 8 points per row or column move, and receives an important 140 point victory bonus.
- Difficile starts at 360 points, loses 12 points per row or column move, and receives a large 280 point victory bonus.
- Each actual row or column rotation increments the move counter.
- A quick-move bonus is added when the player finishes with few movements.
- Quick-move bonus values also depend on difficulty: 15, 30, or 50 points per remaining expected move window.

Reset button behavior
- The Reinitialiser button is visible during gameplay.
- It restores the original grid for the selected difficulty.
- It resets the score to the selected difficulty's starting score.
- It resets the movement counter to zero.
- It keeps the current difficulty selection.

Home button behavior
- The Accueil button is visible during gameplay.
